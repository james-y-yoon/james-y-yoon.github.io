<h2>At the University of Washington</h2>

<h5>Teaching Assistant for ATMOS 211 (Fall 2023)</h5>
<ul>
<li>ATMOS 211 (Climate & Climate Change) is an in-person, introductory climate science class for 100+ environmental science/studies majors</li>
<li>Led four discussion sections every week and designed eight inquiry-based ”labs”. Activities ranged from creating their own visualizations, working with simple climate models, simulating infrared molecular spectra, and interpreting satellite imagery</li>
</ul>

<h5>Teaching Assistant for ATMOS 212 (Spring 2026)</h5>
<ul>
<li>ATMOS 212 (Air Pollution: Societal Impacts and Solutions) is online, asynchronous class for 300+ non-science majors. This course provides a broad, conceptual overview of air pollution, both from science (urban air pollution, ozone hole) and policy points of view</li>
<li>Held 2 office hours/week, monitored the class discussion forum, and updated and organized course logistics and materials</li>
</ul>

<h2>At UCLA</h2>
<hr>

<h5>Pedagogy and Logistics Head Learning Assistant for PHYS 4AL & 4BL (Spring 2020, Summer 2020 (A & C), Fall 2020)</h5>
<ul>
<li>Physics 4AL and 4BL (Mechanics & E&M Laboratory) are the lab components of the mechanics and E&M courses for physics and engineering majors, respectively</li>
<li>Facilitated 4 sections (1 in Spring 2020; 2 in Summer 2020, and 1 in Fall 2020) of 30 students each with virtual, hands-on lab experiments</li>
<li>These activities focused on circuit design and Arduino programming; Python data analysis and visualization using Matplotlib, Numpy, Scipy; and experimental design for their final group projects</li>
<li>Organized a learning assistant initiative to create introductory coding videos for future students</li>
</ul>

<h5>Pedagogy Head Learning Assistant for PHYS 1C (Spring 2021)</h5>
<ul>
<li>Physics 1C (Electrodynamics, Optics, and Special Relativity) is the last of three introductory physics classes for physics and engineering majors</li>
<li>Facilitated two discussion sections of 30 students each week, held office hours with the TA, and organized two midterm review sessions led by the learning assistants</li>
</ul>

<h5>Learning Assistant for CHEM 113A (Fall 2021)</h5>
<ul>
<li>Chemistry 113A (Introduction to Quantum Mechanics) is an upper-division quantum mechanics class for chemistry majors and the second course in the physical chemistry sequence</li>
<li>Facilitated a weekly discussion section and office hours alongside the TA for 50 students </li>
</ul>
